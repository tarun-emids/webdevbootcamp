Our Popular Dishes

Explore some of the most loved dishes from our collection

Jollof Rice

A popular West African dish. It is typically made with long grain rice, tomatoes, tomato paste, onions, salt, and spices. Often served with chicken or beef.

Egusi Soup

A rich and savory soup made with melon seeds,oftern thickend with leafy vegetables like spinach or ugu(fluted pumpkin leaves), and various meats or fish.A staple in Nigerian cuisine.

Pounded Yam with Efo Riro

Pounded yam is a traditional Nigerian dish made by pounding boiled yam into a smooth,dough-like consistency. It's commonly served with Efo Riro, a flavorful Yoruba vegetable soup.
